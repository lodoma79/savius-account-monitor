DEFINITIONS & DATA

How to read the monitor

Who is included?

Customers with exactly 3, 4 or 5 currently active Tomcat 150K or Thunderbolt 300K accounts in the same size group. Only these two products are included. Second Cycle accounts remain in scope and use the same product benchmark. A customer may appear once in each size section. The customer total is deduplicated across both sections.

Profit-target progress

Progress = current source account profit ÷ the fixed product payout target × 100. The confirmed targets are $7,500 for Tomcat 150K and $15,000 for Thunderbolt 300K. Missing or unsupported source values remain unavailable. This measures proximity to the product payout target, not payout eligibility.

Decimal progress from 85% up to, but not including, 86% stays orange. This avoids a gap between the requested whole-number bands. Percentages use every account in the size groups matching the current filters as the denominator, including accounts with unknown progress. A progress filter selects groups containing a matching account; the whole group stays visible and counted. Rounding may make the displayed percentages total slightly above or below 100%.

Payout proximity

A customer's size group is marked High if any account is at 86% or higher; Watch if any account is at 80% to below 86%. If neither applies and any progress is missing, the group is Incomplete. Otherwise it is Lower. This is not a credit score or a prediction of customer behaviour.

Historical outcomes

Confirmed failures count distinct customer accounts explicitly classified Drawdown, Failed or Breached. Other inactive reasons are not estimated as failures. Completed payout accounts count distinct customer accounts with at least one Completed payout in the captured register; reaching a profit target alone does not count. Requested, pending, approved but unpaid, and rejected payouts are excluded. These counts cover the customer’s history across all products, not just the displayed size group. Unknown is not zero.

Refresh & integrity

The collection agent only reads Savius. It updates this external snapshot without changing, editing or deleting source records. The timestamp identifies the data snapshot. A missed collection never makes old data look current.
